# Macrostructures, Category Jobs & Editorial Copy Standards

Full-page layouts and copy choices derive strictly from operational human intent rather than generative layout habits.

---

## The Twenty-One Anti-Convergence Macrostructural Archetypes

Reject the repetitive default AI landing page template (split hero image, three identical icon-topped card columns, numbered process steps, pricing cards, and an email banner above a four-column footer). Instead, choose one of these intentional archetypes matched to the subject matter:

1. **Bento Grid**: Asymmetric modular hierarchy with varied container dimensions and row spans where rhythm stems from scale contrast.
2. **Long Document**: Continuous editorial prose structure resembling a whitepaper, memo, or journal with inline section titles.
3. **Marquee Hero**: Expansive full-viewport statement or singular primary graphic above the fold (`1440 x 900`), flowing directly into structured lists or dense indices below.
4. **Stat-Led**: A single verified, authentic metric anchored at graphic scale with its qualitative proof beneath.
5. **Workbench**: Guided exploration of active software allocating primary space to live interactive component fixtures and genuine UI captures with concise technical notes.
6. **Conversational FAQ**: Inverted interrogative structure addressing real operational questions and constraints via clean disclosure triggers.
7. **Manifesto**: Bold typographic conviction establishing philosophy and design principles before operational specifics.
8. **Photographic**: Full-bleed authentic visual immersion paired with minimalist captions and hairline dividers.
9. **Quote-Led**: Verified stakeholder quotation with exact attribution acting as the primary headline.
10. **Specimen**: High-refinement typographic editorial layout featuring asymmetric columns, hairline rules, generous margins, and purposeful whitespace.
11. **Catalogue**: High-density uniform index displaying variations of design tokens, inventory, or primitives with tabular specs.
12. **Letter**: First-person narrative framed with direct address and personal closure from the engineering team.
13. **Index-First**: Directory-driven navigation where structured rows and metadata form the primary interactive surface.
14. **Narrative Workflow**: Chronological timeline mapping progress across discrete, verified phases.
15. **Split Studio**: Asymmetric diptych pairing editorial argument in one column with live proof in the opposite column.
16. **Feature Stack**: Sticky left rail anchoring context while the right column scrolls through synchronised fixtures or captures.
17. **Type Specimen**: Dedicated typographic showcase highlighting character sets, optical weights, and tabular figures.
18. **Portfolio Grid**: Filterable matrix of real projects, case studies, or completed engineering work.
19. **Map / Diagram**: Spatial architecture anchored by a node-link graph, pipeline flow, or system schema.
20. **Ecosystem Index**: Multi-surface discovery prioritising taxonomy tags, filtering, and search over static declarations.
21. **Component Playground**: Interactive code-and-preview fixtures where live controls, properties, and copy actions form the core surface.

## Category Intent & The Ten Visitor Jobs

Layout anatomy and action placement follow what the visitor came to do:

1. **`subscribe` (Newsletter, Dispatch, Release Feed)**:
   - **Primary Action**: Positioned directly in the hero alongside the latest release edition.
   - **Native Sections**: Sample dispatch or release notes, archive directory, editorial byline.
   - **Closure**: Archive link. This is the only category where a footer email capture is native.
2. **`book` (Studio, Clinic, Workshop, Consultation)**:
   - **Primary Action**: Direct booking selector (date, time, party) in the hero and navigation bar, repeated beside offerings.
   - **Native Sections**: Environmental photography, itemised schedule with real pricing, operating hours.
   - **Closure**: Street address, hours, and direct booking trigger.
3. **`visit` (Exhibition, Venue, Archive)**:
   - **Primary Action**: Operating hours and physical address in the hero or top bar.
   - **Native Sections**: Current exhibition visual, dated calendar, transit and access details.
   - **Closure**: Practical transit directions and hours.
4. **`attend` (Conference, Symposium, Festival)**:
   - **Primary Action**: Ticket reservation in the navigation and hero; date and venue headline the page.
   - **Native Sections**: Chronological schedule index, speaker roster, plain ticket tiers.
   - **Closure**: Registration trigger and travel logistics.
5. **`buy` (Physical Goods, Crafted Objects)**:
   - **Primary Action**: The product itself with price, variants, and direct checkout control in the hero or sticky rail.
   - **Native Sections**: Macro photography, craft process, material specifications, dispatch and return terms.
   - **Closure**: Direct purchase trigger or warranty terms (never tack on a newsletter banner).
6. **`try` (Native App, Desktop Utility)**:
   - **Primary Action**: Direct OS installer downloads directly below the lead statement.
   - **Native Sections**: Live application capture in active use, workflow steps, system compatibility matrix.
   - **Closure**: Download button with release version tag.
7. **`sign up` (Developer Tool, API Platform)**:
   - **Primary Action**: Instant CLI install snippet or direct console sign-up trigger.
   - **Native Sections**: Interactive code fixtures, terminal flows, architectural diagrams, clear pricing.
   - **Closure**: Install snippet repetition or documentation link (never an unrelated newsletter form).
8. **`hire` (Engineering Practice, Architecture Studio)**:
   - **Primary Action**: Direct contact method (email, phone, enquiry link) in top nav and footer.
   - **Native Sections**: Chronological case study index (client, year, outcome), technical method, studio locations.
   - **Closure**: Direct contact coordinates.
9. **`join` (Guild, Membership Cohort)**:
   - **Primary Action**: Application or induction link in the hero.
   - **Native Sections**: Live session previews, timetable, transparent tier rules, onboarding steps.
   - **Closure**: Application CTA or tier summary.
10. **`listen` (Publication, Broadcast, Audio Library)**:
    - **Primary Action**: Current release with inline playback controls.
    - **Native Sections**: Episode/track index with timestamps, credits, broadcast schedule.
    - **Closure**: Release archive or tour schedule.

## Editorial Cadence, Copy Hygiene & The Swap Test

Copy is an architectural material. Synthetic marketing prose weakens visual craft.

### 1. The Editorial Swap Test

Substitute a competitor's name into every headline, lead sentence, and value proposition. If the sentence still sounds plausible and fluent, it has failed the test. Rewrite using concrete domain nouns, physical mechanisms, verified numbers, and operations unique to this product.

### 2. Screen Word Budget Invariant

Communicate authority through typographic scale and working product density rather than explanatory paragraphs:

- **Hero Headline**: Maximum `7 words` (`<= 50 characters`).
- **Hero Subtitle / Lead**: Maximum `12 words` in one crisp sentence.
- **Section Titles**: Maximum `6 words`.
- **Viewport Word Density**: Maximum `80 visible words` inside any single `1440 x 900` viewport.
- **Full Showcase Page**: Maximum `450 visible words` across a complete landing view.

### 3. Banned AI Cadences & Negative Copy Lexicon

- **British English**: Enforce British English spelling across prose, headings, tooltips, and commit messages (`prioritise`, `synchronised`, `colour`, `centre`, `behaviour`, `grey`, `dialogue`, `fulfil`, `judgement`, `catalogue`, `licenced`).
- **Ban Em Dashes**: Never use em dashes in prose or headings; separate clauses with commas, colons, semicolons, or clean periods.
- **Rebuttal Cadence**: Ban pseudo-poetic contrasts ("Not a tool. A revolution.", "Not just software. An experience.").
- **Generic Openers & Colon Templates**: Ban "Built for the modern team", "In today's fast-paced digital landscape", and "Product: the app for...".
- **Negative Copy Lexicon**: Never write `seamless`, `robust`, `empower`, `supercharge`, `elevate`, `effortless`, `unlock`, `unleash`, `next-generation`, `world-class`, `best-in-class`, `mission-critical`, `future-proof`, `game-changer`, `journey`, `reinvent`, `reimagined`.
- **Template Punctuation**: Never join clauses with decorative middle dots (`A · B · C`), glue `→` arrows onto button labels (`Learn more →`), or place decorative dots before plain headings.

## Brand Truth, Factual Authenticity & Licenced Imagery

1. **No Hallucinated Brand Assets ("Memory Hex = Fail")**: Extract brand hex values, OKLCH coordinates, and type stacks strictly from real stylesheets, token definitions, or explicit user instructions.
2. **Factual Integrity & No Synthetic Telemetry**: Ban fabricated testimonials, invented user counts ("Trusted by 10,000+ teams"), and unverified uptime claims ("99.9% uptime"). When social proof is unsupplied, omit the section or render a clear guided slot.
3. **Real Proof Over Mock Browser Divs**: Render working interactive component fixtures or genuine Retina captures instead of HTML fake browser chrome.
4. **Authentic Licenced Photography**: Source imagery strictly from open-licence archives with author attribution in the footer colophon; ban generic stock portrait hotlinks and slop illustrations.

## Micro-Finish & Layout Stability Invariants

1. **Form Helper & Error Slot Stability**: Declare `min-h-[1lh]` on form helper descriptions and validation errors so error toggles cause zero vertical layout shift.
2. **Typographic Balance & Anti-Widow Control**: Set `[text-wrap:balance]` on display/section headings and `[text-wrap:pretty]` on body paragraphs to eliminate trailing single-word stubs.
3. **Container Capping at `1920px`**: Cap wide-viewport containers around `1360px` to `1440px` so outer gutters grow cleanly while reading measure stays bounded.
4. **Themed Browser Surfaces**: Style `::selection`, `caret-color`, and thin `scrollbar-color` controls explicitly for every theme.
